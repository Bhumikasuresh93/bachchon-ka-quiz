Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

App: Bachchon Ka Quiz – Class 1 to 5 • Last updated: 22 November 2026

English

Bachchon Ka Quiz is an educational quiz app for primary school children. We respect children's privacy.

  • The app does not collect, store or share any personal information (no name, email, phone number, location, photos or contacts).
  • The app has no login, no ads, no in-app purchases and no chat.
  • The only thing saved is each class's best quiz score, and it is stored only on the child's own device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the app's data deletes it.
  • The app loads fonts and a display library from public content servers (Google Fonts, cdnjs, Tailwind CDN) the first time it opens. These servers may see a device's IP address as part of a normal web request; we receive nothing from them.
